Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

In short: ashtanga30 does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts and no tracking. Everything the app remembers stays on your own device.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the ashtanga30 web app at ashtanga30.com and the ashtanga30 Android app on Google Play, which is a wrapper around that same website.

Data we collect

None. ashtanga30 has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and we have no server that receives data about you.

What the app stores on your device

To make the app work and remember your preferences, ashtanga30 saves a small amount of information locally in your browser or device storage. This never leaves your device and is not visible to us or anyone else. It includes:

  • Your settings, such as breath speed and whether spoken cues, breath tones, and ambient sound are on or off.
  • A simple record of which days you completed a practice, used to show the last seven days on the Home screen.
  • Standard app-cache data that lets the app load quickly and work offline (the installable/PWA cache).

You can erase all of this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for ashtanga30, or by uninstalling the app.

Sound and network use

Pose art, spoken cues, breath tones, and the optional ambient music track are part of the app and are served only so the app can play them. The ambient track downloads the first time you play it and is then cached on your device. We do not use this to identify or track you.

Permissions

The app keeps the screen awake during a guided practice so it does not dim mid-pose. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

Children's privacy

ashtanga30 does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, you can open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
